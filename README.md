## Case Study: University of Abuja - Department of Computer Science

The Department of Computer Science, University of Abuja (UniAbuja), manages undergraduate academic records for Computer Science students. This portfolio uses the UniAbuja CS Student Result Portal as one consistent case study across all five portfolios.

**System:** Student Result Portal
**Users:** Students, Lecturers, HOD / Exams Officer, Admin
**Data:** Departments, Lecturers, Students, Courses, Semesters, Enrollment, Results
**Operations:** Register courses, assign lecturers, enter CA and Exam scores, compute GPA, generate transcript, identify carry-over courses.
